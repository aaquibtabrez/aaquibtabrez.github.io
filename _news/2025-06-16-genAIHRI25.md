Our work, *Improving Human-Robot Interaction via a Population of Synthetic Human-like Teams*, has been accepted to the [GenAI-HRI Workshop at RSS 2025](https://sites.google.com/view/gai-hri/home)!

This work explores how Quality Diversity (QD) and LLM-powered agents can be used to generate and study diverse teaming behaviors.
